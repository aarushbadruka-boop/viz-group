Introducing our company

Built in Hyderabad.
Relied on across the grid.

Viz is a leading ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer of power factor correction, motor run, motor start and power electronic capacitors in India.

We are constantly working with our customers with the goal to offer them innovative engineering solutions, reliable service, and high-quality products. Viz focuses on technologically driven applications, such as power quality, industrial, renewable energy, consumer electronics, automotive and power electronics.

Viz has 2 state-of-art manufacturing facilities spread over 50,000 sq. ft., both located in Hyderabad, India, which are equipped with the latest and best available world class machines and testing equipment. Viz has complete R&D expertise for the development of new products, which are type tested and validated at Viz’s fully equipped in-house laboratory. Our quality has taken VIZ capacitors to customers in Europe, the Middle East, CIS and Southeast Asia.

Viz PQS Private Limited

Power electronic capacitors,
made in India.

Viz PQS Private Limited has been established in Hyderabad to manufacture metallised polypropylene film as well as MPP film capacitors used in power electronics, motor run, power factor correction, DC capacitors and more.

Viz PQS metallises its own film, to keep control over the quality, design, timely supply and pricing of metallised films. The company produces high-end capacitors whose films are designed specifically for each customer requirement. Our in-house R&D team develops these proprietary designs — making in-house metallisation all the more important to protect the company’s intellectual property.
