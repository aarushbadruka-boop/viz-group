About VIZ

Built in Hyderabad.
Relied on across the grid.

An ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer of metallized polypropylene film capacitors.

From two state-of-the-art, air-conditioned plants spanning more than 50,000 sq. ft. in Jeedimetla, Hyderabad, VIZ designs and builds capacitors for power quality, industrial, renewable energy, consumer electronics and automotive applications — backed by in-house R&D and type-testing laboratories.
